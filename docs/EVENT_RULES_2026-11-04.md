# Global AI Builder's Challenge rules checked November 4, 2026

- Event: November 21–28, 2026.
- Submission deadline: November 28, 2026 at 3:00 PM Pacific.
- Eligibility: students aged 13+; solo or teams up to six.
- New projects and meaningful improvements to existing projects are allowed. Teams must disclose pre-existing work and clearly identify what they add during the event.
- Submission: public GitHub repo, 2–4 minute demo video, Devpost project description, complete team roster, and accurate AI/development disclosures.
- AI integration is strongly encouraged and contributes to judging, but is not mandatory. The rubric weighs problem/practicality (20), AI integration/development (30), functionality/reliability (30), and design/UX (20).

**Current baseline:** ClearTask v1.0.1 was built before the event. The final submission must identify this as pre-event work and document actual event-period changes separately in the changelog and event log.

Official rules: https://global-ai-builders-challenge.devpost.com/rules
